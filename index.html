About

This page is a live, collaborative build by the Vidhya Vidhai team with Stratos and CoLab. Each person claims a card and makes it their own, right from the terminal, by talking to Claude Code.

Tonight

Make your card yours — three changes from home

Open Claude Code inside the wall folder and say what you want, one sentence at a time. It edits your block and publishes it. Ideas:

Add a line under my name about my programme and what we do. Change the background to a colour I like. Add a link to our website. Then publish it.
  • Where: your terminal, cd ~/Desktop/vv-wall then claude. Windows: cd $HOME\Desktop\vv-wall then claude.
  • Done looks like: your card on the live page with at least three things that are yours.
  • Push rejected? Say: someone else pushed, pull their work and push mine again.
  • Stuck? Screenshot it and show Claude — then reply on the thread, and Sahariar will unblock you.
